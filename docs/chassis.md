# Chassis: housing, frame and body

Generated by `fusion/SENNEP_ChassisGenerator/`. Robot size **800 × 650 mm** (`RobotLength` × `RobotWidth`), wheelbase 550 (set by Claude, **not confirmed by Mikkel**), wheel centres at x = ±287 (track ≈ 574). Screenshots: `docs/skelet/`. The first sketch is in `docs/skelet_udkast_v1.html`.

## Corner housing ("pod" in code, "housing" to Mikkel)
One printed part per corner. It holds the wheel bearing and the motor, and it prints **standing on its motor end** (169 × 118 × 89 mm), so layers are perpendicular to the axle and every hole is vertical.
- **Bearing:** KFL003 17 mm pillow block in a tight pocket, held by a printed **bearing keeper** (6 mm, pins in the flange holes, 2× M3 × 19 + washer).
- **Housing v2 (current, `print/housing_v2/`):** the keeper screws come **from the back**. Heads sit in a Ø7.6 counterbore with 6 mm of plastic under them, the brass inserts sit in the keeper, and a Ø10 tool channel runs out through the motor end, clear of the motor. The set screw is reached from below (Ø12, `Chassis_ToolDia`). Collar lugs are rotated to 90° (`Chassis_LugAngle`) because their channels were on the screw line.
- **Floating motor:** a printed collar (6 mm, 4× M4 × 12 into the motor's own M4 × 6 threads on PCD 35) has 2 triangular lugs in channels in the motor tube (0.1 mm). The tube carries the gearbox (Ø42.4 per the datasheet). The motor only floats axially, and the bearing carries the load.
- **Motor lock:** not solved. The motor is held axially only by the coupling set screw.
- Solid: ~236 g at 3 walls / 15 % infill. The hollow variant (`make_hollow`) uses ~5 g more.
- Wheel clearance ≥ 2 mm all round (round wheel well, R = wheel + 2 mm), ground clearance 23.6 mm.

## Ladder frame (skeleton v3.3)
All beams are inverted U-profiles (top on the plate, open downwards so bolts are reached from below), 6 mm walls.
- **Side rails** |x| 196–256, 50 mm high, two halves per side joined at y = 0. The front halves are 222 mm long and lie diagonally on the plate.
- **4 cross beams** at y = ±60 and ±170 (34 × 40), each in two halves joined at x = 0, holes every 30 mm on top.
- **Bumpers** front and rear, 3 segments each, bolted to the housings' outer faces. **Diamond channels** at x = ±60 run through bumpers and cross beams, so a rod can pass from front to back.
- **LiDAR mast** (printed standing) on the cross beams at y = ±60.
- **Joints:** each has a V-key (90° ridge in one part, groove with 0.1 mm clearance in the other) plus bolts. Ridges run up to the plate and grooves run out at the bottom, so nothing faces down.
- **Fasteners:** printed **M8 × 1.25 bolts** (P-M8 × 26, thread cut by Mikkel) in all beam joints, the mast foot and the battery plate. Bought M3 only in the corners and at the bearing.
- **Battery** plate on cross beams y = −170/−60 with a cut-out for the mast foot.
- Test print of one joint plus an M8 bolt: `print/skelet_test/` (`build_test_print()`).

## Body v0 ("50 Shell", visual only)
Octagonal from above (`Shell_Corner` 70, so the ToF sensors sit on the angled corners), sides and rear chamfered towards the top (`Shell_Chamfer` 35), sloped front, flat top z = 250, 3 mm wall, sitting on the frame (z 73.6). About 650 × 800 × 176 and ~2.8 kg PETG if solid. Cut-outs: wheel wells R62, LiDAR hole, 7" screen (156 × 88) and camera Ø14 in the sloped front, head- and tail-lights Ø22, 8 ToF windows Ø10, grilles for 2 intake fans + 2 speakers in the sides and 2 exhaust fans at the back, E-stop Ø22 at the top rear left, triangular grid in the top.
- **LiDAR limit:** the scan plane sits at z ≈ 285 (foot on `BodyTop` 258). With up to 2° pitch error the shell must stay below z ≈ 264. The LiDAR margin is 17 mm.
