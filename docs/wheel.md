# Wheel

A 100 % printed mecanum wheel, generated by `fusion/SENNEP_WheelGenerator/`. Current print set: `print/testhjul_v3.1/` (guide in Danish: `LÆS MIG.md`).

## Current design (v3.1, Fusion "SENNEP V2 v27", generator unchanged since v18)
- **Ø106.2 mm**, 9 rollers at 45°, ~64 mm wide. Mecanum X layout needs left- and right-handed wheels (FL = RR, FR = RL; `HAND` in the generator). **Only one hand exists so far.**
- **Rollers:** Ø21.5 PETG cores (work without sleeves; the core stands 2.0 mm proud, 3.2 mm with the optional TPU sleeve). They run on short printed pin stubs from both rims (`Wheel_StubA` 8, `Wheel_StubB` 6, tapered), with a flat thrust ring (`Wheel_ThrustRing` 2.5) and an end gap of 0.4. Ø22 is the hard limit at 9 rollers: larger sleeves touch their neighbours.
- **Rims:** Rim A faces the motor, Rim B faces out. Rim B is twisted on along 3 helical grooves on Rim A's Ø30 hub tube, following the same screw path as the pins (~42°).
- **Axle** (printed PETG, 119.3 mm), from outside in: head Ø42 → 18 mm hex through the wheel (locks rotation, so no separate lock) → spacer Ø26 × 12.7 (`Axle_ToBearing`) → Ø16.9 × 19.5 bearing section (perfect fit, do not change) → Ø16.9 × 19 coupling (`Axle_CouplingL`) with a D-hole for the Ø8 motor shaft (Ø8.4) and an M3 set screw in a captured nut. Prints standing on its head.
- **Spacer:** separate part with a bayonet into Rim A: triangular lugs on one 45° profile, 60° turn to lock, 0.2 mm clearance, verified free of overhang.
- **Key fits:** `Wheel_KeyClear` 0.1/side, `Wheel_TubeClear` 0.15, `Axle_HexClear` 0.2, `Axle_SetScrewHole` 4.3.
- **Assembly ring** (`assembly_ring.py`, `print/testhjul_v3.1/samlering/`): a cradle that holds the rollers while Rim B goes on, because the rollers fell off Rim A's short pins. It sits at r ≥ 50 so the wheel lifts straight out. PETG, ~65–71 g.

**Assembly order:** set screw a few turns into its nut → rollers on Rim A in the ring → twist Rim B on → hex axle through from outside → spacer bayonet → bearing → motor shaft + set screw. The full housing assembly order is in `print/testhjul_v3.1/LÆS MIG.md` and `print/housing_v2/LÆS MIG.md`.

## Load estimate
At Ø106 the motor's rated torque gives ~33 N per wheel → **~50 kg total is the design target** (≈ 90 kg on hard floor and ≈ 48 kg on carpet going forward; top speed ~0.8 m/s at no load). Printed PETG pins hold for the robot alone, not for a child standing on it (that was only a party joke). Details: `motor-and-power.md`.
