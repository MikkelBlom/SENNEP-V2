# Future ideas

Outside the current scope. Move an idea to `FEATURES.md` when it is picked up.

| Idea | Origin | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Docking station | 2025-04 | Always-on 940 nm IR beacons on the dock, VS1838B receivers on the robot. Rotate until front/rear receivers balance, centre sideways, drive in. Charge via two metal plates + spring contacts. Parts (IR LEDs, receivers) already bought. |
| Swappable shell | 2026-10-05 | Everything needed to drive lives on the skeleton, and the shell only carries detachable modules (screen, camera, fans, speakers, lights) with plugs. Interface: 6 shell brackets, one connector plate, max height set by the LiDAR mast. |
| Accessory rod through the robot | 2026-10-06 | The diamond channel at x = ±60 already allows a rod from front to back. An earlier 20 × 20 tow-hitch receiver was removed in v3.3. |
| AI Camera models | 2025-05 | Emotion recognition was tried on the IMX500 |
| GPS outdoor use | 2025-12 | NEO-6M is bought and is to be included. The robot is indoor-only today. |
| Sound direction | — | The microphone modules only give level/threshold. Real direction would need different hardware. |
| Ride-on strength | 2026-10-05 | A child standing on it was a party joke. It would need steel pins and SLS PA12 rims, not FDM PETG. |
| Laser-cut parts | 2026-10-04 | The library has a free laser cutter. 100 % print is preferred. |
| Second bearing per wheel | 2026-10-05 | Plan B if the self-aligning KFL003 lets the moment reach the motor shaft |
